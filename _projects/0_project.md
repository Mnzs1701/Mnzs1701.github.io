Built at the Actor Labs Act-athon with Gaurav Sethia and Siddharth Ghodasara, where the project
placed in the **top 5**.

We fine-tuned Physical Intelligence's π0.7 vision-language-action model into a single
language-steerable policy that controls two very different robots working together: an SO-101 arm
and a custom butter-passing robot. The same policy drives both embodiments, and natural language
instructions steer what each robot does.

The butter-passing robot is my own design and build, inspired by the butter robot from Rick and
Morty: a mobile base carrying two arms that move up and down, made to collaborate with the SO-101.
