Carnegie Mellon MRSD capstone project, sponsored by Meta Reality Labs.

We created a human following drone that avoids obstacles, built on the DJI SDK and ROS2 running on
a Mavic 3 Enterprise. The drone keeps a person in frame while navigating around obstacles, and the
captured imagery drives a photorealistic avatar.

We trained a model on synthetically generated avatar models and demonstrated a real time full body
codec avatar driven from the drone's view.
